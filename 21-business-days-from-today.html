What does "21 business days" mean?

Twenty-one business days equals three full work weeks. In calendar terms, this is roughly one calendar month (28-31 days depending on holiday placement).

  • From Monday → the Monday four weeks out (28 calendar days)
  • From Tuesday → the Tuesday four weeks out (28 calendar days)
  • From Wednesday → the Wednesday four weeks out (28 calendar days)
  • From Thursday → the Thursday four weeks out (28 calendar days)
  • From Friday → the Friday four weeks out (28 calendar days)

If a US federal holiday falls within that window, the result shifts forward by one day for each holiday encountered.

When is "21 business days" used?

  • Mortgage processing: initial underwriting turnaround.
  • Custom manufacturing: made-to-order product lead times.
  • Permit applications: building and zoning permit review cycles.
  • PTO approval: advance notice for extended leave requests.
