Understanding 21 Business Days

21 business days equals about 4 weeks plus 1 day. This is a common timeframe used in shipping, contracts, and administrative processes. When someone says "21 business days," they mean 21 working days — Monday through Friday — excluding public holidays. The exact calendar date depends on which day you start counting and whether any holidays fall within the period.
