Business Days Between Two Dates

How many working days fall between any two dates? Excludes weekends and public holidays.

How this calculator works

It counts every weekday (Monday through Friday) between your two chosen dates that is not a public holiday in the selected country. The count includes the start date if it is a business day, and excludes the end date.

Common use cases

  • Project planning: how many working days do you have between now and a deadline?
  • Payroll: count business days in a pay period.
  • Leave tracking: how many working days did you take off?
  • Contract billing: days worked between invoice dates.
  • Shipping lead time: business days between order and delivery dates.
