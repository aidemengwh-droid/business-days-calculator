Shipping Time Calculator

When will your package arrive? Enter the shipping speed in business days and get the estimated delivery date, excluding weekends and holidays.

Shipping speeds explained

  • Overnight (1 business day): next working day delivery. Order Monday, arrives Tuesday.
  • Express (2 business days): order Monday, arrives Wednesday.
  • Expedited (3 business days): common for priority shipping. Order Friday, arrives Wednesday.
  • Standard (5 business days): one full work week. Most common free shipping option.
  • Economy (7 business days): budget shipping. Roughly 9-11 calendar days.
  • Ground (10 business days): cross-country ground shipping. About 2 calendar weeks.
  • Freight (14 business days): large item and international economy shipping.

How to read your delivery estimate

The date shown is the business day your package is expected to arrive. It already skips weekends and public holidays. For example, if you order on a Friday with 3-business-day shipping, your package arrives Wednesday (not Monday, because Saturday and Sunday do not count).
