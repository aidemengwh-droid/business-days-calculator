What does "90 business days" mean?

Ninety business days is roughly 4-5 calendar months (about 18 work weeks). This is a key milestone for long-range planning, contract expirations, and regulatory deadlines.

  • From Monday → approximately 4-5 calendar months ahead
  • From Any weekday → varies by 3-7 days depending on holiday density in the period

If a US federal holiday falls within that window, the result shifts forward by one day for each holiday encountered.

When is "90 business days" used?

  • Contract termination: many service agreements require 90 business days notice.
  • Regulatory reviews: FDA, EPA, and other agency review windows.
  • Large-scale projects: Phase 1 delivery milestones.
  • Relocation planning: international move coordination timelines.
