Understanding 90 Business Days

90 business days equals exactly 18 full work weeks. This is a common timeframe used in shipping, contracts, and administrative processes. When someone says "90 business days," they mean 90 working days — Monday through Friday — excluding public holidays. The exact calendar date depends on which day you start counting and whether any holidays fall within the period.
