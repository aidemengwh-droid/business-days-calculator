What does "60 business days" mean?

Sixty business days is approximately three calendar months (about 12 work weeks). This is a common milestone for long-term project planning, contract durations, and extended processing timelines.

  • From Monday → approximately three calendar months ahead
  • From Any weekday → varies by 2-5 days depending on holidays in the period

If a US federal holiday falls within that window, the result shifts forward by one day for each holiday encountered.

When is "60 business days" used?

  • Probation periods: many employment contracts set 60-business-day review milestones.
  • Large purchase delivery: furniture, custom vehicles, and specialty equipment.
  • Immigration processing: preliminary review windows for certain visa categories.
  • Construction permits: major renovation and new-build approval timelines.
