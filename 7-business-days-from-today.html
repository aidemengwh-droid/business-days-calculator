What does "7 business days" mean?

Seven business days means one full work week (Monday-Friday) plus two more working days. In calendar days, this usually spans 9-11 days depending on where weekends fall.

  • From Monday → the Wednesday of the following week (9 calendar days)
  • From Tuesday → the Thursday of the following week (9 calendar days)
  • From Wednesday → the Friday of the following week (9 calendar days)
  • From Thursday → the Monday two weeks out (11 calendar days)
  • From Friday → the Tuesday two weeks out (11 calendar days)

If a US federal holiday falls within that window, the result shifts forward by one day for each holiday encountered.

When is "7 business days" used?

  • Standard processing: many government services quote 7-10 business day turnaround.
  • Payroll cycles: weekly payroll with a 7-day clearing window.
  • Order fulfillment: custom or hand-made product lead times.
  • Insurance claims: initial review windows often quoted at 5-7 business days.
