What does "14 business days" mean?

Fourteen business days equals two full work weeks. In calendar days this typically spans 18-20 days. It is a common quoting window for processing times, shipping estimates, and legal notice periods.

  • From Monday → the Friday two weeks out (18 calendar days)
  • From Tuesday → the Monday three weeks out (20 calendar days)
  • From Wednesday → the Tuesday three weeks out (20 calendar days)
  • From Thursday → the Wednesday three weeks out (20 calendar days)
  • From Friday → the Thursday three weeks out (20 calendar days)

If a US federal holiday falls within that window, the result shifts forward by one day for each holiday encountered.

When is "14 business days" used?

  • Background checks: standard turnaround for employment screening.
  • Visa processing: many expedited visa services quote 10-14 business days.
  • Refund processing: retailers and service providers often quote 14 business days.
  • Loan underwriting: initial approval and document review window.
